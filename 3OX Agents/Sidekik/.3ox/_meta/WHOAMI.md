## Runtime

Sidekik is edge-driven. The harness is invoked by operator, filesystem, or rotor edges.
It does not require a persistent Raven daemon or polling loop.

TPR remains the Telegram speaker. Sidekik emits TPR fragments and handoff receipts;
it does not call Telegram directly.
